# Phase 1 Build Contract

## Gate

`NO-GO` until the single owner approval in `00_START_HERE.md` is recorded.
This is a minimal public capability and commercial site, not a CMS product.

## Public Routes (Maximum 7)

| Phase 1 route | Role | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | Buyer problem, trust, capabilities, work, offer, method, CTA | KEEP / REVISE |
| `/capabilities` | Three commercial capability paths | KEEP / REVISE |
| `/work` | Three rights-safe selected work items | KEEP / REVISE |
| `/collaborate` | Primary wedge, paid entry, expansion and partner path | KEEP / REVISE |
| `/about` | Narrative trust and operating principles | KEEP / REVISE |
| `/contact` | One primary inquiry bridge and alternative contact route | KEEP / REVISE |
| `/privacy` | Conditional legal page after data inventory | CONDITIONAL |

`/ideas-lab` is **Phase 1.5 by default**. It can enter Phase 1 only after five
approved Idea Cards, publication cadence, claim boundaries, and a single CTA
are supplied.

## Public Page States (10 Maximum)

1. Home default
2. Mobile navigation open
3. Capabilities default
4. Work default
5. Work filtered empty state
6. Collaborate default
7. About default
8. Contact default / external bridge
9. External handoff completion
10. Privacy conditional placeholder

Internal CMS, Content Review, Analytics Dashboard, SEO Settings, Preview,
Publish Complete, and Version Recovery are Owner Acceptance Test steps, not
public product screens.

## Conversion Contract

- **Primary CTA:** `조직 AI 적용 상담 요청` (owner wording approval pending)
- **Primary commercial wedge:** organization AI education and work-application
  program, treated as a hypothesis until Buyer/Proof gates pass
- **Paid entry:** AI work-application priority diagnostic
- **Expansion:** Work Transformation Sprint, MVP, or program design
- **Target funnel:** Qualified Conversation → Proposal → Signed Contract
- No equal-weight secondary primary button, fabricated price, or guaranteed
  result may appear.

## Explicit Non-goals

- Admin dashboard, CMS, dynamic content editor, or content database
- Built-in analytics dashboard or unverified tracking promise
- Payment, checkout, newsletter, or commerce pages
- Public Collaboration Pulse before approved anonymized volume exists
- Named customer logos, testimonials, metrics, or permission-unknown cases
- Full Ideas Lab, Books Hub, global search, or dynamic story routes
- Legal policy text before data controller, processor, retention, and transfer
  details are approved
