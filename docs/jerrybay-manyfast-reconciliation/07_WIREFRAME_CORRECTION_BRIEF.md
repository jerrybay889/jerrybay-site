# Wireframe Correction Brief

The desktop and mobile exports remain quarantined. Their visual structure is
reference material only; copy, dates, claims, legal text, and contact details
must not be promoted.

## Home

- **Keep:** editorial hero, capability-led framing, selected work region,
  method narrative, responsive shell.
- **Revise:** order as Hero → Trust → 3 Capabilities → 3 Work → Offer
  Hierarchy → Method → CTA. Make the buyer problem and outcome visible before
  taxonomy. Give the commercial CTA one visual priority.
- **Remove:** unverified numbers, organizations, completion claims, equal
  primary buttons, and dense Ideas/Work grids.

## Capabilities

- **Keep:** capability taxonomy and related story links.
- **Revise:** show three Phase 1 commercial capabilities and connect each to a
  buyer problem, safe output, and the same primary CTA.
- **Remove:** detail screens without approved story content and invented
  customer proof.

## Ideas Lab

- **Keep:** concept status language as a future asset.
- **Revise:** default to Phase 1.5; publish only five approved Idea Cards with
  problem, insight, current state, next expansion, and claim boundary.
- **Remove:** fabricated adoption, traction, or market numbers.

## Work

- **Keep:** selected work and rights-safe display pattern.
- **Revise:** limit Phase 1 to three approved items; each needs role,
  contribution boundary, evidence artifact, permission state, and current
  status.
- **Remove:** named clients, outcomes, pilots, and completed labels without
  evidence.

## Collaborate

- **Keep:** collaboration route cards.
- **Revise:** one Primary Wedge, one Paid Entry, and one Expansion path, with
  partner/content paths secondary.
- **Remove:** unapproved prices, contract terms, guarantees, and generic
  multi-CTA menus.

## About

- **Keep:** narrative bio, principles, and current focus.
- **Revise:** use truthful experience language and separate personal role from
  organization or former-employer outcomes.
- **Remove:** unsupported career metrics, rankings, or client attribution.

## Contact and Privacy

- **Keep:** low-friction external handoff and error alternative.
- **Revise:** select the actual approved tool before publishing fields,
  consent, storage, retention, controller, processor, or cross-border text.
- **Remove:** invented privacy@ address, team name, dates, one-year retention,
  and form-success promises.

## Mobile Correction Baseline

- Body text: 15px minimum target; avoid 11–12px gray copy.
- Touch targets: at least 44px with visible focus.
- Contrast: validate normal text against WCAG AA before release.
- Long-scroll: reduce card count and move Offer/Proof before taxonomy.
- CTA: one primary style; secondary actions use text or a lower-emphasis link.
- State labels: remain readable and never imply proof by color alone.
